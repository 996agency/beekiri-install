# Beekiri trademark policy

Beekiri is free to use (see [LICENSE](./LICENSE)). The **name and logo are not**. This page explains how you can talk about Beekiri and name the things you build for it, without suggesting that your product is Beekiri or is made by 996agency.

## What the trademarks are

- The names **Beekiri** and **Beekiri CMS**
- The **Beekiri logo** (the "beekiri" wordmark) and the **bee mark**
- The names of Beekiri's own features and extensions, such as **BeeQueen**, **BeeCookies**, **BeeSEO** and **Bee Forms**

They belong to **996agency**.

## You can, without asking

- Say that a website is **"built with Beekiri"** or **"powered by Beekiri"**, and link to Beekiri.
- Name your theme or extension **"… for Beekiri"**, for example *"Aurora — a theme for Beekiri"* or *"Booking for Beekiri"*.
- Write articles, tutorials, videos and courses about Beekiri, and use the name in their titles.
- Offer **Beekiri hosting**, **Beekiri development** or **Beekiri support** as a service, as long as it is clear that you are not 996agency.
- Show the Beekiri logo **unchanged** when you refer to Beekiri itself, for example on a page listing the platforms you work with.

## Please don't

- **Rebrand Beekiri.** Replace or remove the Beekiri name or logo in the admin, sign-in, setup, installer or update screens, or ship Beekiri under another name.
- **Name your product as if it were Beekiri**, for example *"Beekiri Pro"*, *"Beekiri Studio"* or *"NewCMS (based on Beekiri)"*.
- **Use the logo or the bee mark as part of your own logo**, product icon or app icon, or change the logo (colours, shape, proportions).
- **Use "Beekiri" in a domain name, company name or social handle** in a way that suggests it is official, such as `beekiri-themes.com` or `@beekiri_official`.
- **Suggest that 996agency made, endorses or supports** your product when it does not.

## Need something else?

Partnerships, co-branding, official integrations or anything not covered here: contact 996agency. We are happy to help when it's clear to everyone who made what.
